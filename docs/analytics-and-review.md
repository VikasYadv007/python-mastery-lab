# Progress and revision

## Explore Progress

Choose a practice type, lecture, topic, and time range. Summary metrics and the
attempt journal use those filters. Coding results reflect your self-assessment;
MCQ results are graded automatically. All daily grouping uses IST.

- **Learning pulse:** daily attempts and accuracy, results donut, activity calendar,
  active days, streaks, and a daily attempt target. The target is remembered per
  learner for this browser session, including when you navigate to another page.
- **Topics & difficulty:** compare outcomes by topic and accuracy by difficulty.
  Click a topic bar to update the spotlight, or use its selector. Start a Smart Mix
  practice session from the spotlight or open a coding challenge.
- **History & exports:** inspect recent attempts, export every filtered attempt,
  resume a saved quiz, or download the whole profile's history and backup.

Hover over charts for exact values. Native chart controls offer data inspection,
PNG downloads, and fullscreen views. The activity graph adapts to short histories
and shows at most 90 days. The calendar shows up to 84 days. Summary metrics and
exports cover the full selected range, even when the chart window is shorter.

A streak counts consecutive IST days with at least one attempt. The current
streak stays active through yesterday. The best streak respects the selected time
range. Current mastery is shown separately: three consecutive correct results
count as mastered, regardless of the chart's selected time range.

Attempts referencing unavailable packs are excluded from analytics but remain in
full exports. Reinstall the original pack to include them again. Empty time ranges
show no accuracy rather than inventing a zero score.

## Use the revision studio

Filter by type, lecture, topic, difficulty, and search terms. Choose a queue:

| Queue | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Needs another try | The most recent result was incorrect or needs practice. |
| Due now | The scheduled review timestamp has arrived, including overdue items. |
| Bookmarked | Saved questions, including ones you have not attempted. |
| Recovered mistakes | A question had mistakes before, but its latest result is correct. |
| All studied | Installed questions you have attempted. |

Queues overlap. The overview totals refer to installed content; the controls
below narrow the practice queue. Sort by mistake count, oldest review date, or
topic. Focused revision uses the highest-priority matching MCQs, up to the chosen
size, and shuffles them within the new session. Coding challenges open separately.

The question spotlight supports previous/next browsing, single-question retry,
bookmarks, saved revision notes, answer explanations, and an attempt timeline.
Revealing an explanation does not grade the question or change its schedule.

**Mistake history** preserves earlier incorrect attempts even after recovery. It
respects content filters but ignores the practice-queue selector. Inspect a past
mistake or export all matching incorrect attempts. **Review forecast** separates
items due now from later today and the next seven IST dates. Use its action to
switch directly to the due queue.

Charts, filters, previews, and navigation do not mark questions correct. New
results are saved only through the existing quiz submission or coding self-grade
actions. A new result updates the revision schedule and may move a question
between queues; it never erases its earlier attempts.
